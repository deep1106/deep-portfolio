01 — FORWARD-DEPLOYED AUTOMATIONREAL RETAIL OPERATIONS

Less catalog busywork.
More room for the real work.

For Shayona Store, I built production workflows on self-hosted n8n for product documents, missing catalog images, and barcode extraction into Google Sheets.

Self-hosted n8nGoogle DriveGoogle SheetsAI image generation
How the work fit the operation

Shayona is a real saree and ethnic-wear retailer. The workflow set supported live catalog operations rather than a demo: automating document creation, surfacing missing product imagery, and moving barcode data into a sheet the team could use.

I connected Drive, image-generation steps, and Google Sheets, with retry handling for transient integration failures. Customer workflow exports and connected account identifiers stay private; the live storefront is public.
