HOW I APPROACH A NEW PROBLEM

Close to the work.
All the way to live.

Good engineering starts with the people doing the work and finishes when the solution fits into their day.

    Listen before designing

    Understand what happens today, what gets in the way, and what a better day would look like.

    ⌕
  2. 02
